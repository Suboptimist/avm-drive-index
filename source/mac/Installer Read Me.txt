AVM Drive Index

1. Drag “AVM Drive Index.app” to the Applications shortcut.
2. Open the app from Applications.
3. Click “Turn On” once to start automatic drive indexing.

Your private catalogue is kept in:
~/Library/Application Support/AVM Drive Index

Note: To distribute this app without macOS security prompts, it must be
signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.
